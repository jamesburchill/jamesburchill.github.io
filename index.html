About James

A career built on curiosity

I enjoy understanding how things work, making them better, and sharing what I learn.

Over four decades, I’ve founded companies, led engineering teams, built software platforms, written three bestselling books, and taught more than 35,000 learners worldwide. I’ve also taught at Sheridan College and McMaster University.

More about James

I’m Principal & Systems Architect at James Media Group and CTO at Tooljar. My product work focuses on keeping commitments, conditions, and follow-through in view, with enough notice to act when things drift.

A recurring interest across my writing and product work is drift: how commitments, conditions, and systems move away from what we intended, and how we recognize meaningful changes with enough time to respond.

For a different way into those questions, read The Blind Squirrel Method, my free illustrated fable about luck, skill, and the stories we sell.
