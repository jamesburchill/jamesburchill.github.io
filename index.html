About

The titles change.
The work remains.

I’m a Business Systems Architect. Technology is a central thread in how I turn business problems into working solutions.

Over nearly four decades, I have founded companies, led engineering teams, architected enterprise platforms, written and taught, and helped organizations navigate technological change. Software engineering, publishing, teaching, marketing, and entrepreneurship all inform how I approach a problem.

Current engagements include CTO at Tooljar, alongside selected business systems and technology projects. My career includes senior software leadership with Givex and Shift4.

Other chapters include three bestselling books, courses reaching more than 36,000 learners, and teaching computer science, marketing, social media, and public relations at Sheridan College and McMaster University. My independent work includes Driftinel™, Because Drift Happens™, and The Vault.
