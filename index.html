About

My career spans nearly four decades across software engineering, entrepreneurship, publishing, and teaching. I’ve founded companies, led engineering teams, built software platforms, and written three bestselling books. I’ve also taught at Sheridan College and McMaster University.

I enjoy understanding how things work, making them better, and sharing what I learn. A recurring interest across my writing and product work is drift: how commitments, conditions, and systems move away from what we intended, and how we recognize meaningful changes with enough time to respond.
