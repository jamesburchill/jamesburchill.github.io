About

The titles change.
The work remains.

Technology has never been my end goal. Building systems that remain coherent as they grow has.

For more than four decades, I have founded companies, led engineering teams, architected enterprise platforms, written and taught, and helped organizations navigate technological change. Software engineering, publishing, teaching, marketing, and entrepreneurship all inform how I approach a problem.

My work spans technical leadership, independent ventures, and selected private engagements. I currently serve as CTO at Tooljar while also developing my own products and working directly with other founders and organizations. My career includes senior software leadership with Givex and Shift4.

My independent work includes Driftinel™, Because Drift Happens™, and The Vault—products, principles, and field notes shaped by that broader experience.
