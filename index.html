About

My career spans nearly four decades across software engineering, entrepreneurship, publishing, and teaching. I’ve founded companies, led engineering teams, built software platforms, and written three bestselling books. I’ve also taught at Sheridan College and McMaster University.

I enjoy understanding how things work, making them better, and sharing what I learn. A recurring interest across my work is how businesses and their systems change over time—and how we keep them connected to what they were meant to achieve.
