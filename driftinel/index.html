Frequently asked questions

The essentials.

How the managed service is intended to work, what you control, and what is still in development.

What is Driftinel?

Driftinel is being developed as a secure, turnkey managed service. We provision a server for your business, connect selected channels, monitor agreed activity, and notify you when drift is detected. Business memory and search provide the context behind those findings.

Do I need to keep my laptop on?

No. The managed service is designed to run on a server provisioned for your business. Monitoring continues independently of your laptop or browser, subject to the server and source connections remaining operational.

What does turnkey mean?

We provision, configure, and manage the server environment, connect agreed sources, and set up monitoring and notifications. You approve what the service can access and what it should watch. Service scope and support arrangements will be defined before activation.

What can Driftinel connect to?

Integrations will connect selected business sources, such as email, documents, and project systems, where supported. The initial connector set is still in development; the sources available to each client will be confirmed during setup.

What can I ask it to remember or find?

Record decisions, commitments, and context, then ask questions across that memory and approved connected information. Answers are intended to include sources you can inspect.

How will Driftinel notify me?

Notifications will be sent through an agreed channel when Driftinel detects drift in the activity being monitored. They will point you to the change and its supporting evidence, so you can review it and decide what to do. Notification delivery options are still in development.

How will I know whether monitoring is working?

The service is being designed to distinguish findings needing attention, completed checks with no exceptions found, and incomplete visibility. A failed connection or unfinished check must be visible; silence alone is not an all-clear.

Where will my information be kept?

Business information will be held in the server environment provisioned for your service. Hosting location, access, and data-handling requirements will be agreed during setup.

Will AI processing run on the server or use a cloud provider?

Processing arrangements are still being defined. Server-based inference and external model services are options to assess against the client’s requirements. Any external processing and its data access would need to be explicitly agreed.

Does Driftinel take action automatically?

The initial service is intended to observe and inform. You review the evidence and decide what happens next.

Why is it called Driftinel?

Driftinel combines “drift” and “sentinel.” The name reflects its purpose: to keep important context within reach and watch for meaningful changes while there is still time to respond.

Is Driftinel available yet?

Driftinel is in private development. The managed service, integrations, and monitoring coverage are still being developed and validated. Purchasing and early-access applications are not yet open.

The thinking behind Driftinel

Recognize change.
Keep the purpose in view.

Driftinel was founded by James Burchill, Systems Architect, CTO, and bestselling author.

It grew from a recurring pattern I’ve encountered across business and technology: important context gets scattered, small changes accumulate, and the gap between what was intended and what is happening becomes harder to see.

Driftinel applies the thinking behind Because Drift Happens™ to the information a business owner chooses to bring into view. The aim is to help people recognize meaningful changes while there is still time to respond.
