Frequently asked questions

The essentials.

What Driftinel does, how it treats your information, and what participation means.

What is Driftinel?

Driftinel is a local-first business memory assistant in development. It helps you remember decisions, find answers across selected information, and notice supported changes—so less of your business has to live in your head.

What can I ask it to remember or find?

Capture decisions, commitments, and context, then ask questions across those memories and your selected files. The current app reads text PDFs, Word documents, Markdown, plain text, CSV, and HTML. Scanned-document OCR is not yet available.

What works today?

The private development app supports explicit memories and corrections, selected-folder indexing, semantic search, cited answers, knowledge review, and initial drift rules. OCR, email and calendar connections, broader drift coverage, and customer-ready installers remain development work.

What does deselecting a source do?

It removes that source from current search and stops its indexing. Retained history is separate from source selection. Pausing indexing keeps the last known content searchable.

Why is it called Driftinel?

Driftinel combines “drift” and “sentinel.” The name reflects its purpose: a business memory that stays useful when you ask and watches your back in the background. Memory, semantic search, and drift detection are its three first-class features.

Does Driftinel run on my computer?

Yes. Driftinel is designed to run locally on a computer you control. Development is currently focused on Mac, with Windows support planned. Customer-ready installers are still in development.

Does Driftinel scan my entire computer?

No. The current app indexes only the local folders you select. You can deselect sources and manage exclusions. Additional service connections remain future work.

Where is my information kept?

Driftinel keeps selected business information and observations on your own device unless you deliberately choose otherwise.

Does Driftinel use external services?

Driftinel is local-first. Any capability that requires an external service is clearly disclosed and deliberately enabled.

Does Driftinel take action automatically?

No. Driftinel informs; you review the evidence and decide.

What is a Detector Pack?

A Detector Pack gives Driftinel a focused way to examine selected information for a particular kind of change, pattern, or open loop.

What is the Founding Edition?

It is the planned early-access program for solopreneurs and small-business owners who want to help shape useful memory, search, and drift detection. Availability and participation details are shared directly with selected applicants.

Does Driftinel require a subscription?

No mandatory subscription is planned for the purchased local application. The first year includes updates and standard support. Optional support packs are CAD $99/year thereafter. Your eligible version remains usable if you do not renew.

When does my licence become permanent?

Under the planned purchase terms, paying the first-year price upfront gives you a permanent licence immediately after confirmed payment. With a twelve-payment plan, the licence becomes permanent after all twelve payments are completed. Instalment plans require periodic internet access to verify payment status; overdue payments may suspend use until the account is brought current. Final payment and grace-period terms will be confirmed before purchase.

Is the monthly option a subscription?

No. Founding Edition is CAD $29/month for 12 months (CAD $348 total); Standard Edition is CAD $39/month for 12 months (CAD $468 total). Purchase payments end after the twelfth payment, when you receive a permanent licence. Optional support packs are CAD $99/year thereafter. You can keep using your purchased version without renewing.
