Important problems begin quietly

The signal was already there. It was scattered.

Small businesses rarely lack information. The clues are spread across email, calendars, documents, notes, project tools, spreadsheets, browser-based systems, and the owner’s memory.

The problem is not that the information does not exist. The problem is that nobody is continuously connecting it.

Illustrative examples -- not customer data

What am I missing?

Driftinel uses cautious language, shows the evidence it found, states what remains uncertain, and leaves the decision with you.

IllustrativeOpen loop

Follow-up may be overdue

Evidence

  • A proposal was promised in a meeting note five days ago.
  • No related outgoing email was found.
  • No completed task references the proposal.
  • The opportunity remains active.

Interpretation

Driftinel found evidence of a commitment, but no matching evidence that it was completed.

Possible next step Review the opportunity and decide whether follow-up is required.

IllustrativeRelationship change

Customer activity has changed

Evidence

  • This customer normally replies within one business day.
  • The last two messages remain unanswered.
  • No meeting is currently scheduled.
  • Recent project activity is below the previous baseline.

Interpretation

No single change is conclusive. Together, they suggest that the relationship may deserve attention.

Possible next step Review the relationship and decide whether direct outreach is appropriate.

IllustrativeProject delivery

Deadline may be drifting

Evidence

  • The deadline is approaching.
  • Related file activity has slowed.
  • An unresolved decision remains open.
  • No recent completion evidence was found.

Interpretation

Based on the information currently available, the activity pattern may not support the planned deadline.

Possible next step Review the blocked decision and current delivery plan.

Who Driftinel is for

For people whose business lives on their computer.

Driftinel is for solopreneurs and small-business owners who manage customers, commitments, projects, files, notes, and follow-ups across several tools -- while carrying much of the context in their own head.

  • The business is run primarily through a computer.
  • Important information is spread across several places.
  • The owner handles many roles without a dedicated operations team.
  • Missed details can affect revenue, trust, or delivery.
  • Privacy, ownership, and evidence matter.
  • You want help noticing, not software deciding for you.

A deliberate focus

Driftinel is built for owner-led businesses. It is not employee-monitoring, centralized enterprise-surveillance, or autonomous business software.

Background operation

Quiet until something deserves attention.

Driftinel is not another application demanding constant attention. It works with the information you select and surfaces only the observations that may be worth reviewing.

Your computer does not need to run continuously. Driftinel always shows how current its information is.

Know what Driftinel knows

Every observation makes its limits visible.

  • What information supports the observation
  • How current that information is
  • What may be missing or incomplete

You remain in control of what Driftinel may watch.

Driftinel connects evidence and raises specific observations. It does not replace the judgement or accountability of the person running the business.

Frequently asked questions

The essentials.

What Driftinel does, how it treats your information, and what participation means.

What is Driftinel?

Driftinel is local-first early-warning software for solopreneurs and small-business owners. It connects signals across user-approved business information and surfaces evidence-backed observations.

Does Driftinel run on my computer?

Yes. Driftinel is a local-first product for a computer you control.

Does Driftinel scan my entire computer?

No. Driftinel watches only the folders, files, and connected sources you explicitly select.

Where is my information kept?

Driftinel keeps selected business information and observations on your own device unless you deliberately choose otherwise.

Does Driftinel use external services?

Driftinel is local-first. Any capability that requires an external service is clearly disclosed and deliberately enabled.

Does Driftinel take action automatically?

No. Driftinel informs; you review the evidence and decide.

What is a Detector Pack?

A Detector Pack gives Driftinel a focused way to examine selected information for a particular kind of change, pattern, or open loop.

What is the Founding Edition?

It is early access for solopreneurs and small-business owners who want to help shape useful, trustworthy observations.

Does Driftinel require a subscription?

Driftinel is software you own, without a mandatory subscription to keep using the version you purchase.
