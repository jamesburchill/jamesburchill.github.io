Frequently asked questions

The essentials.

How the managed service works, what you control, and what is still in development.

What is Driftinel?

Driftinel is a turnkey managed service in development that helps business owners keep their promises. It monitors the business channels each client chooses and flags emerging drift with supporting evidence, giving owners time to act before missed deadlines or overlooked details become costly mistakes.

Do I need to keep my laptop on?

No. Driftinel runs on a server we provision for your business. Monitoring continues independently of your laptop or browser, subject to the server and source connections remaining operational.

What does turnkey mean?

We provision, configure, and manage the server environment, connect agreed sources, and set up monitoring and notifications. You approve what the service can access and what it watches. We define the service scope and support arrangements with you before activation.

What can Driftinel connect to?

Driftinel connects to selected business sources, such as email, documents, and project systems, through supported integrations. We confirm the sources and integrations for your service during setup.

What can I ask it to remember or find?

Record decisions, commitments, and context, then ask questions across that memory and approved connected information. Answers include sources you can inspect.

How often does Driftinel check?

Driftinel checks your chosen business channels every 14 minutes. That’s just over 100 checks a day, so you can get on with yours.

How does Driftinel notify me?

Driftinel sends notifications through the channel you agree with us during setup. Each notification points you to the change and its supporting evidence, so you can review it and decide what to do.

How do I know whether monitoring works?

Driftinel distinguishes findings that need attention, completed checks that find no exceptions, and incomplete visibility. It flags failed connections and unfinished checks, so you can see where monitoring needs attention. Silence alone is not an all-clear.

Where does Driftinel keep my information?

Driftinel keeps your business information in the server environment we provision for your service. We agree on hosting location, access, and data-handling requirements with you during setup.

Does AI processing run on the server or use a cloud provider?

We assess server-based inference and external model services against your requirements and agree on processing arrangements with you during setup. You explicitly approve any external processing and the information it can access.

Does Driftinel take action automatically?

Driftinel observes and informs. You review the evidence and decide what happens next.

Why is it called Driftinel?

Driftinel combines “drift” and “sentinel.” The name reflects its purpose: to keep important context within reach and watch for meaningful changes while there is still time to respond.

Is Driftinel available yet?

Driftinel is in development. We develop and validate the managed service, integrations, and monitoring coverage. Purchasing and early-access applications are not yet open.
