What is Driftinel?

An extra set of experienced eyes.

Driftinel is being developed as an intelligent business observation system. Instead of waiting for you to search, it notices patterns in the information your business already produces and surfaces what deserves attention.

It works above the tools you already use. Knowledge stays where it belongs; Driftinel builds understanding across it.

01

Observe

Pay attention to normal business activity across the systems already in use.

02

Connect

Recognize relationships among small signals that look unrelated in isolation.

03

Explain

Surface an evidence-backed observation only when it justifies interruption.

What it notices

Small changes.
Meaningful patterns.

These are not reports or dashboards. They are observations that connect evidence and explain why it matters now.

Customer experience

“Three customers have reported the same issue this week.”

Commitments

“You promised a proposal that hasn’t been sent.”

Revenue

“Revenue from a major client has been steadily declining.”

Organizational memory

“You’ve solved this problem before.”

Delivery

“A project is beginning to drift off schedule.”

Operational risk

“Several small issues are pointing toward a larger operational problem.”

Why this matters

Business problems rarely appear overnight.

They begin as a delayed response, a forgotten commitment, a slowing customer, or an emerging trend. Individually, each change seems insignificant.

Together, they become lost clients, missed revenue, project failures, and unnecessary stress. Driftinel is designed to notice while there is still time to respond.

The objective is not more notifications. The objective is fewer surprises.

The difference

Awareness is the product.

AI, embeddings, knowledge graphs, and search are implementation technologies. Customers should not need to understand them.

Not a chatbotConversation is an interface. Observation is the responsibility.
Not searchSearch retrieves what you request. Driftinel identifies what you did not know to ask.
Not business intelligenceHistory is evidence, not the destination. Attention belongs on what is changing now.
Not automationDriftinel improves judgement. It should rarely perform consequential actions itself.
Not another databaseExisting systems remain the sources of truth. Driftinel builds understanding above them.
Not a decision makerDriftinel provides evidence, context, and possible consequences. The human decides.

Human accountability

Artificial intelligence should inform decisions.
Not own them.

Driftinel is intended to extend human attention—not replace executive judgement. Every observation should show its evidence and make the reasoning understandable. Responsibility remains with the person making the decision.
