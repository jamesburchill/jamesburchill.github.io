Three first-class features

One business memory.
Three ways it works for you.

Remembering, searching, and detecting drift each provide value on their own. They become more useful when they share the same context.

01 · Memory

Keep what matters.

Ask Driftinel to remember decisions, commitments, facts, and context—even things that were never written into a formal document.

“Remember: the customer approved the revised delivery date.”
  • Capture information in the moment.
  • Bring approved documents into your business memory.
  • Keep attribution, corrections, and history.

Less context carried in your head.

02 · Search

Find the meaning.

Search your files, captured memories, and drift observations using the ideas you remember. Semantic search finds relevant information even when your words differ from the original.

“Which earlier proposal solved a problem like this?”
  • Ask natural questions across selected information.
  • Find related work, decisions, and commitments.
  • Follow citations to the supporting source.

Useful answers, even on days without alerts.

03 · Drift detection

Notice the departure.

While Driftinel is running, supported detectors compare recorded activity with expectations. They raise concerns only when the evidence and attention rules justify it.

“This forecast is later than the date we agreed.”
  • Work quietly in the background.
  • See what changed and why it matters.
  • Review, dismiss, snooze, or resolve a concern.

Drift detection examples · illustrative, not customer data

What am I missing?

Driftinel uses cautious language, shows the evidence it found, states what remains uncertain, and leaves the decision with you.

IllustrativeOpen loop

Follow-up may be overdue

Evidence

  • A proposal was promised in a meeting note five days ago.
  • No related outgoing email was found.
  • No completed task references the proposal.
  • The opportunity remains active.

Interpretation

Driftinel found evidence of a commitment, but no matching evidence that it was completed.

Possible next step Review the opportunity and decide whether follow-up is required.

IllustrativeRelationship change

Customer activity has changed

Evidence

  • This customer normally replies within one business day.
  • The last two messages remain unanswered.
  • No meeting is currently scheduled.
  • Recent project activity is below the previous baseline.

Interpretation

No single change is conclusive. Together, they suggest that the relationship may deserve attention.

Possible next step Review the relationship and decide whether direct outreach is appropriate.

IllustrativeProject delivery

Deadline may be drifting

Evidence

  • The deadline is approaching.
  • Related file activity has slowed.
  • An unresolved decision remains open.
  • No recent completion evidence was found.

Interpretation

Based on the information currently available, the activity pattern may not support the planned deadline.

Possible next step Review the blocked decision and current delivery plan.

User-selected business information

Your sources. Your boundaries.

Driftinel does not need access to everything. You choose the information that contains the business context you want it to understand.

Nothing is watched simply because it exists on your computer.

Files and folders

Choose folders containing text PDFs, Word documents, Markdown, plain text, CSV, and HTML. Your originals stay where they are and remain unchanged.

Connected sources

Email, calendar, and other business-tool connections are future work. Selecting a folder today does not give Driftinel access to those services.

Your boundaries

Select or deselect sources, pause or resume indexing, request a rescan, and manage exclusions. The Mac menu bar keeps those controls close at hand.

Who Driftinel is for

For people whose business lives on their computer.

Driftinel is for solopreneurs and small-business owners who manage customers, commitments, projects, files, notes, and follow-ups across several tools -- while carrying much of the context in their own head.

  • The business is run primarily through a computer.
  • Important information is spread across several places.
  • The owner handles many roles without a dedicated operations team.
  • Missed details can affect revenue, trust, or delivery.
  • Privacy, ownership, and evidence matter.
  • You want help noticing, not software deciding for you.

A deliberate focus

Driftinel is built for owner-led businesses. It is not employee-monitoring, centralized enterprise-surveillance, or autonomous business software.

Frequently asked questions

The essentials.

What Driftinel does, how it treats your information, and what participation means.

What is Driftinel?

Driftinel is local-first early-warning software in development for solopreneurs and small-business owners. It is designed to connect signals across user-approved business information and surface evidence-backed observations.

What are Driftinel’s three features?

Memory keeps decisions, commitments, and approved business information. Search retrieves it by meaning and answers with sources. Drift detection quietly notices supported departures from expectations and raises justified concerns.

What works today?

The private development app supports explicit memories and corrections, selected-folder indexing, semantic search, cited answers, knowledge review, and initial drift rules. OCR, email and calendar connections, broader drift coverage, and customer-ready installers remain development work.

What does deselecting a source do?

It removes that source from current search and stops its indexing. Retained history is separate from source selection. Pausing indexing keeps the last known content searchable.

Why is it called Driftinel?

Driftinel combines “drift” and “sentinel.” The name reflects its purpose: a business memory that stays useful when you ask and watches your back in the background. Memory, semantic search, and drift detection are its three first-class features.

Does Driftinel run on my computer?

Yes. Driftinel is a local-first product for a computer you control.

Does Driftinel scan my entire computer?

No. The current app indexes only the local folders you select. You can deselect sources and manage exclusions. Additional service connections remain future work.

Where is my information kept?

Driftinel keeps selected business information and observations on your own device unless you deliberately choose otherwise.

Does Driftinel use external services?

Driftinel is local-first. Any capability that requires an external service is clearly disclosed and deliberately enabled.

Does Driftinel take action automatically?

No. Driftinel informs; you review the evidence and decide.

What is a Detector Pack?

A Detector Pack gives Driftinel a focused way to examine selected information for a particular kind of change, pattern, or open loop.

What is the Founding Edition?

It is the planned early-access program for solopreneurs and small-business owners who want to help shape useful memory, search, and drift detection. Availability and participation details are shared directly with selected applicants.

Does Driftinel require a subscription?

Driftinel is software you own, without a mandatory subscription to keep using the version you purchase.
