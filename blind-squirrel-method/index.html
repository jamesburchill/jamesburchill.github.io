Welcome to Bracken Wood

Success makes a good story.
Does it make a good method?

Pip has sharp eyes and a wandering attention span. When a lucky landing turns up a magnificent acorn, Mallow the magpie spots something even more promising: an audience.

What follows is a woodland business fable about luck, skill, missing evidence, hidden advantages and the appeal of advice from someone with a very full basket.

Across nine illustrated chapters, Pip and his friend Hazel learn to ask better questions. The book ends with a practical checklist and a worksheet for examining the next success claim that comes your way.
