MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a standard that lets AI assistants connect to information and tools provided by other services.

A working demonstration of AI access

This service demonstrates how a business can make selected information available directly to customers’ AI assistants. Here, it connects you to my published writing, featured book, and public information.

What could this do for your business?

An MCP service gives customers a way to use your business information within the AI conversations they already have. They can ask a question, find relevant material, and follow the source without having to navigate every page themselves.

  • Help customers find answers. Let their assistants search your product information, documentation, policies, and support resources.
  • Provide useful source material. Return relevant passages and links that customers can check, helping assistants answer from your published information.
  • Support business tasks. With the appropriate integrations and permissions, an assistant could check availability, prepare a quote, or book an appointment.

This website is a working example. Your assistant can search my writing, browse topics, retrieve articles, and find resources. A business could apply the same approach to its own information and services. Operational tasks such as quotes and bookings would require additional integrations; this demonstration provides public information only.

Customers need a compatible assistant and a connection to your service. Adding MCP alone does not guarantee that assistants will discover or recommend your business.

Start with a useful customer question and the information needed to answer it. That gives you a practical way to decide what your service should offer.

No separate account with me or API key is required. Your assistant must support remote MCP using Streamable HTTP. Setup options and availability vary by application and plan. The address above is for an MCP client; opening it as a normal webpage will not start a chat.

Public information only

The service cannot access private files, customer records, email, or calendars, and it cannot send messages or book appointments. Public content refreshes on demand, with up to five minutes of caching.

Requests are processed on my server. The application does not retain your prompts or search terms; temporary connection information is used for rate limiting. Your AI provider’s own data policies still apply. Please do not send private information.
