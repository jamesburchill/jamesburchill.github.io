Public MCP service

MCP privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy covers the public MCP service at mcp.jamesburchill.com, operated by James Burchill. It does not cover your AI provider’s service or other websites you visit.

What the service receives

Your assistant sends the tool name and the arguments needed for your request, such as search keywords, a topic, an article identifier, or pagination settings. The server also receives connection information, including an IP address and request headers. Depending on your assistant, this may identify its provider’s server rather than your device.

The service does not ask for an account, password, payment details, or access to private files, email, calendars, or customer records. It does not request your full conversation. Your assistant decides which tool arguments it sends. Please do not include private or sensitive information in those arguments.

How information is used

Tool arguments are used to search or retrieve published writing, the public professional profile, and featured books and resources. Temporary connection information is used to limit request rates and protect availability. The service cannot send messages, make bookings, or make commitments on James’s behalf.

Storage and retention

The application does not store search terms, tool arguments, or request bodies in a database or application request log. They are processed in memory for the request. Rate-limit counters use IP-derived identifiers in memory for a one-minute rate window and expire through routine cleanup or when the process restarts.

Copies of public source content are reused from memory for up to five minutes before refresh. Expired copies may remain in the bounded memory cache until replaced, evicted, or the process restarts. The cache contains published source material, not a saved history of your searches. Server lifecycle and diagnostic messages may be retained in system logs under the host’s log-retention settings. The supplied service configuration does not enable web access logging or log request bodies.

Providers and sharing

Hosting and network providers necessarily process connection information to deliver the service. Public content is retrieved from James’s websites and his public GitHub writing repository. The service constructs those source requests from published records; it does not forward your search query or conversation to a separate AI model.

The application does not sell request data or use it for advertising, tracking, or model training. It sets no cookies and includes no analytics or tracking scripts. Your AI provider processes your conversation and tool results under its own policies. Links to other websites are governed by those websites’ policies.

Your choices and contact

You can stop using the service or disconnect it in your assistant’s settings at any time. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@jamesburchill.com. Because the application does not keep a searchable history of requests, it cannot retrieve or delete a history it has not stored.
